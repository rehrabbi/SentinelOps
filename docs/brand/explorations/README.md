# Logo explorations

Five directions for the SentinelOps mark: an abstract watchful eye that combines an eye, a shield and a radar sweep.
Each is a 64 × 64 SVG with no text, scripts or external references. The ink is `currentColor`,
so the mark takes the colour of the text around it, and the one accent is `#1596d6`, the ice blue that clears 3:1
contrast on both the dark (`#0a1020`, 5.75:1) and the light (`#f6f8fc`, 3.10:1) background.
The final accent per theme is the brand board's call.

- `index.html`: contact sheet (open it in a browser), each mark at 180, 32 and 16 px on both backgrounds.
- `contact-sheet.png`: the same sheet as an image; the last tile in each panel is the real 16 px render magnified ×4.
- Regenerate both from `frontend/`: `node scripts/render-explorations.mjs`.

| | Name | Concept | Motion idea | At 16 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Ward | A shield whose crest is a raised brow; the eye is cut out of the shield, with an ice-blue pupil. | Blink: the cut-out closes to a line and reopens, then the pupil brightens once. | Strongest silhouette; the eye stays legible. Weakness: shield-plus-eye is a familiar security trope, so it is the least surprising. |
| B | Gaze | Two crescent lids, sharp at the corners, around an ice-blue iris. The eye itself, wide awake. | Look: the iris glides left, right and back to centre, as if checking the room. | Reads as an eye. Weakness: the tapered lid corners soften to grey, and it sits closest to a stock "eye" icon. Wide, so it uses only about half the square. |
| C | Facet | An eye in straight lines: two chevrons as lids (the lower one a shield point) and a diamond pupil. | Lock: the chevrons close onto the pupil like a shutter and part again; the diamond ticks a quarter turn. | Holds well after thickening; diagonals anti-alias slightly. Weakness: can read as a gem or diamond before it reads as an eye. |
| D | Visor | An eye cut into three horizontal bands, like a visor slot; the pupil is a lit ice-blue block. | Scan: the bands light top to bottom in turn, then the pupil settles. | Middle band and pupil are crisp; the pointed top and bottom caps soften. Weakness: it is the most "tech", so the scan must stay calm or it tips toward the hacker cliché. |
| E | Scope | A radar scope seen as an eye: the ring is the iris, the centre the pupil, the ice-blue sector the beam. | Sweep: the beam turns once around the ring and rests at the top. | Boldest at 16 px; fills the square. Weakness: static, it can read as a donut chart or a loading ring, and its eye-ness depends on the centre dot. |
