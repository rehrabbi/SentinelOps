01 · The mark

Facet, refined

Your pick, kept as drawn: two chevron lids and a diamond pupil. The changes are about precision. Every horizontal edge now sits on the 16 px pixel grid, the slit between the lids is a little wider, and the pupil is a little larger, so the eye still reads at favicon size.

Night watch

Daylight watch

Upper lid

A chevron, 10 units thick on a 64 grid. Its flat ends close the eye's outer corners.

Lower lid

The same chevron turned over: the point of a shield. The two lids are mirror images, so the mark is calm and balanced.

Pupil

A 16-unit diamond in the signal colour, the only colour in the mark. It is 4 px wide at 16 px.

Grid

Edges at y 28 and 36 land on whole pixels at 16 px, so the slit is two crisp pixels. Clear space: one pupil width on every side.

03 · The eye in motion

Lock, wake, and a quiet scan

Transform and opacity only. Loops here so you can watch them; in the app each plays once, at the moment named. If your device asks for reduced motion, this page shows them still.

Lock

The lids close onto the pupil like a shutter, the diamond ticks a quarter turn, the lids part. Says "secured": after sign-in succeeds, when a session is verified.

Close 270 ms ease-in-out · tick 360 ms ease-out · open 360 ms ease-out

Wake

The eye starts shut, the lids part and the pupil ignites, turning into place. The sign-in moment, once on load.

Part 560 ms ease-out · pupil 420 ms ease-out, 220 ms after

Idle scan

The pupil checks left and right, then sends one quiet ping. Landing hero only, never on working screens.

8 s loop · glide ease-in-out · ping 1.6 s ease-out

Reduced motion

With reduce motion on: no travel, no turn. Lock and wake become a 200 ms fade; the idle scan does not run.

200 ms linear opacity, or nothing

The signal problem

The exploration found that the icy #7dd3fc is only 1.57:1 on the light background, while #1596d6 clears both but looks dull at night.

The answer: one hue, two depths

Ice blue at hue 199 to 202, light on dark and deep on light. Night: #86d8ff, 12.0:1. Day: #0a6ea8, 5.1:1. Both are safe for text, not just for shapes.

#86d8ff on night#0a6ea8 on day

Where there is no theme

A standalone logo file or an unknown background uses #1596d6, which clears 3:1 on both (5.7 and 3.1). The favicon picks the right depth from the system theme.
